Privacy Policy

Littafin Waƙoƙi (Hausa Hymn Book) · Effective date: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hymnestry (“we”, “us”, “our”), operated by TechOveride in Abuja, Nigeria, handles information when you use the Littafin Waƙoƙi mobile app (the “App”). We built the App to help people worship, and we collect as little information as we can.

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, phone number or location.
  • Your favourites, songs you compose and reading settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand app usage and deliver updates.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Hymns you mark as favourites.
  • Songs you compose or add in the App.
  • Your settings, such as font size, colours, theme, night mode and “keep screen awake”.
  • Whether you have purchased Pro (so ads stay off).

This information is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. Information collected automatically

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you use the free version, the App shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your settings, ads may be personalised. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Buying Pro removes ads from the App.

App analytics and configuration (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used (for example, which screens are opened and how often the App crashes or is updated). Firebase may collect a device-specific app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version, approximate country and usage events. We do not use it to identify you personally.

We use Firebase Remote Config to deliver settings, such as the latest app version and the address for hymn updates, without you needing to download a new version of the App.

See the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Hymn updates

The App periodically downloads corrections and new hymns from our server at hymnestry.techoveridehub.com. Like any website, our server may briefly record technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request. We use this only to deliver and secure the service.

App updates

The App uses Google Play’s in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. This is handled by Google Play.

3. Information you choose to send us

When you use Report a Bug, Help, Contribute a Tune, Custom Church App or Contact Us, the App opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you press send. We receive:

  • your email address and anything you write or attach;
  • for bug reports, basic diagnostic details added to the message: your platform (e.g. Android), app version, whether night mode is on, the active theme and the time of the report. You can see and remove these before sending.

We use these emails only to reply to you, fix problems and improve the App.

4. Purchases

The optional Littafin Waƙoƙi Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We receive only confirmation that the purchase was made; we never receive your card or bank details. Your purchase is linked to your Google account, so you can restore it on a new device with Restore Purchases.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the App and its features, including hymn and app updates.
  • To show ads in the free version.
  • To understand usage, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed below, who process it on our behalf or under their own privacy policies, or when required by law.

7. Data retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for the period set in our Firebase settings, after which it is deleted. Emails are kept for as long as needed to help you and then deleted. Google AdMob and Google Play keep data according to Google’s own policies.

8. Security

Information sent between the App and Google or our server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

9. Children

The App is a hymn book suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission). Because the App has no accounts, most information is on your device and you control it directly:

  • Delete app data: clear the App’s storage or uninstall it.
  • Advertising: reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings.
  • Emails and other requests: contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the App.
